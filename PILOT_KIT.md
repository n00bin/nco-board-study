# NCO Board Study Guide: Unit Pilot Kit

A one-page guide for a leader (squad leader, PSG, 1SG, CSM) who wants to know whether the app helps Soldiers at their board.
Free, no accounts, nothing is collected automatically: Soldiers choose what to share.

**App:** https://n00bin.github.io/nco-board-study/ (Android: Chrome → Install app. iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.)

## What you need
- 10 or more Soldiers going to a SGT or SSG board in the next 1–3 months (more is better; 20+ gives a clearer answer).
- A pilot code you make up, for example `2-505-NOV`.
- 10 minutes at the start and a way to collect results (group chat or email).

## Steps
1. **Kick-off (10 minutes).** Each Soldier installs the app, then in the app:
   - picks their board (SGT or SSG) on the first screen,
   - sets their board date (More → Settings),
   - enters the pilot code (More → Unit Pilot).
2. **Study.** Soldiers follow **Today's plan** on Home. It paces them to the board date and switches to final-week review automatically.
3. **Sponsor boards.** Each Soldier runs **at least two** sponsor boards: Mock Board → *Send a board to my sponsor*. The sponsor runs it on their own phone and sends the results (score, bearing grades, notes) back.
4. **Squad board (optional).** Soldiers send their progress link to the group chat and add each other to the **Squad Board** (More → Squad Board & Sponsor).
5. **After each board.** On Home, the Soldier taps **How did your board go?** (Recommended / Not recommended / Postponed) and then **Share it**. The shared text includes the pilot code, the board-ready score before the board, Board Core cards studied, and Mock Boards run.
6. **Compare.** Count the recommendation rate for pilot Soldiers and, if you can, for Soldiers who went to the same boards without the app. Note sponsor boards and cards studied: does more practice line up with better results?

## What to report back (optional)
Send the totals (no names needed) to the app's issue page so the app can show real-world results:
pilot code, number of Soldiers, number recommended, average cards studied, average Mock Boards, average sponsor boards.

## Ground rules
- The app is a study aid. Your board memo and local SOP always come first.
- Nothing about a Soldier is shared unless that Soldier taps Share.
- The app is free and unofficial; it is built from current Army publications with paragraph citations on every card.
