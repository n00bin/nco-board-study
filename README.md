# NCO Board Study Guide

A phone study app for the **E-5 / E-6 Army promotion board**, built from 30+ Army publications plus the 2019–2026 Army Directives.
It installs to your Android or iPhone home screen, runs **fully offline**, and keeps your progress on your phone —
no account, no app store, nothing to sign up for.

**Live app:** https://n00bin.github.io/nco-board-study/

## What's inside

- **Board 101** — what a promotion board is: eligibility, who sits on the board, what they look for, how they
  vote, promotion points and cutoff scores (AR 600-8-19, March 2026). First track in Learn Mode.
- **What Would You Do?** — 47 situational leadership scenarios (SHARP, suicide prevention, substance abuse, EO,
  hazing, Article 31, illegal orders, casualty care, ...) with a model answer, the governing regulation, and
  the board's follow-up questions. Mock Board mixes them into every board.
- **Sponsor mode & record-and-replay** — hand the phone to your sponsor (they see the answer and grade you), or record
  your own answer and play it back to hear your delivery. Recordings stay on the phone.
- **Know Your Army & Your Job** — prompts and notes for your unit, your MOS, your installation, and current events.
- **Personal questions** — coaching for "Why should we recommend you?", "Biggest weakness?" and more, a sample opening
  statement, and a "What the board room is like" page built from AR 600-8-19.
- **More staple subjects** — property accountability (AR 735-5, 2026), uniform placement (DA PAM 670-1), M249/M240/pistol
  basics, and more Army history.
- **Today's plan & Ready? check** — one daily plan (next lesson, reviews, new cards, a Mock Board) and a board-readiness
  score with your weakest subjects. With a board date set, every card you've studied comes back for one last review
  in the final days, and board week adds a Final-Week Review of your weakest cards.
- **Board date countdown** — set your board date in Settings; Home shows days left and a daily Board Core pace.
- **Staple board subjects** — customs & courtesies (AR 600-25, 2026), flags & streamers (AR 840-10, 2025),
  drill & ceremony (TC 3-21.5), awards (AR 600-8-22, 2026), the NCOER (AR 623-3, 2025), and NCO history &
  duties (TC 7-22.7, 2025 + The Army Blue Book).
- **Board Core (start here)** — about 400 must-know questions boards ask most, each with a one-line **why it matters**, pulled from every
  publication. Mock Board, the night-before cram, and Learn Mode's Board Core track use it by default;
  "All Publications" keeps the full deep set (~2,000 cards) for when you want detail.
- **Learn Mode (start from scratch)** — a guided path of bite-sized lessons that **teach** the material
  first (with a plain-English overview of each publication), then **practice** it. Begins with the
  fundamentals (Army Values, ranks, chain of command, creeds, Code of Conduct), then each publication.
  Tracks which lessons you've completed. *Use this if you're new; use the drills below once you know it.*
- **Flashcards with spaced repetition** — grade each card **Again / Good / Easy**; the app reschedules
  it automatically (miss it → comes back soon, know it → comes back later) and surfaces what's *due*.
- **Quiz** — multiple-choice, scored, with instant right/wrong feedback. Pick 10, 20, or all questions.
- **Mock Board** — a timed oral-board simulation. **Full board** mode rehearses the whole thing: report in, "tell the
  board about yourself," the questions (with the board's **follow-up questions sprung on you** after you answer),
  then report out. Answer out loud, self-grade, and get a score, a bearing & delivery check, and your **weakest subjects**.
- **Drive Mode** — hands-free auto-play audio: reads the question, pauses for you to answer aloud, reads
  the answer, advances. Keeps the screen awake. Study during PT or the drive in.
- **Type answers** — fill-in-the-blank recall (toggle on the flashcard screen), plus **Leech focus**
  (cards you keep missing) and a **Night-before cram**.
- **Read-aloud** — the phone speaks the question and answer.
- **Board Day Prep** — a fill-in **chain-of-command roster** (becomes quiz questions), an **AR 670-1
  uniform inspection checklist**, a **how-to-report-to-the-board** guide, and an **opening-statement** draft.
- **My Cards** — add your unit's smart-book questions (command policy, local SOPs).
- **Daily goal & streak**, **Browse** by publication, full-text **Search**, and **Backup/restore**
  (export your progress to a file and move it to a new phone).

Content covers **30+ publications plus the 2022–2026 Army Directives** with **~2,000 cards** — most fact-checked page-by-page against the
source PDFs, with a page citation on each card (the Army Values, Code of Conduct, MDMP/TLP, warfighting
functions, SHARP reporting, the AFT and Combat Field Test, the General Orders, the Soldier's & NCO Creeds, and much more).

## Get it on your Android phone (easiest way — about 3 minutes)

Phones can only "install" a web app when it's served over a secure (`https`) web address, so we put the
`app` folder online for free first. The simplest option needs **no account**:

### Option A — Netlify Drop (no account, instant)
1. On your computer, open **https://app.netlify.com/drop** in a browser.
2. Drag the **`app`** folder (this folder) onto the page.
3. It uploads and gives you a link like `https://random-name.netlify.app`.
4. On your **phone**, open that link in **Chrome**.
5. Tap Chrome's **⋮ menu → "Add to Home screen"** (or "Install app"). Confirm.
6. Open it from your home screen like any app. It now works **offline**.

> The free Netlify link can expire after about an hour unless you make a free account. If you want a
> permanent link, make the free account when it prompts you, or use Option B.

### Option B — GitHub Pages (permanent, free, needs a free GitHub account)
1. Create a repository and upload the contents of the `app` folder to it.
2. In the repo: **Settings → Pages → Build from branch → `main` / root → Save**.
3. After a minute you get a permanent link like `https://yourname.github.io/your-repo/`.
4. Open it on your phone in Chrome and **Add to Home screen** (same as above).

### Preview on your computer first (optional)
From inside the `app` folder, run one of these, then open the printed address:
```
python -m http.server 8000
```
Go to `http://localhost:8000`. (Service-worker offline mode only activates over `https` or `localhost`,
so test offline behavior on the phone after installing.)

## How to study

- Tap **Flashcards** → pick **Board Core** (recommended), All Publications, or one subject → tap the
  card to flip → grade it **Missed it / Got it / Easy**. Missed cards come back soon; easy ones much later.
- Tap **Quiz** → pick a subject → choose how many questions → answer.
- The home screen and each subject show a **mastery bar**. A card counts as "mastered" once you've
  gotten it right enough times that it's scheduled a week or more out.
- **More → Backup & restore → Danger zone** lets you reset progress (export a backup first).

## Adding or changing study content

All questions live in one plain-text file: **`data/content.js`**. You don't need to touch any other file.
Each card looks like this:

```js
{ id:"ar600-20-01", pub:"ar600-20", topic:"Chain of Command",
  q:"What Army Regulation covers the Army Command Policy?",
  a:"AR 600-20",
  ref:"AR 600-20",
  choices:["AR 600-20","AR 670-1","AR 350-1","AR 27-10"], answer:0 },
```

- `pub` must match one of the publication `id`s listed at the top of the file.
- `choices` + `answer` are **optional** — include them to write a custom multiple-choice question
  (`answer` is the position of the correct choice, starting at 0). Leave them out and the quiz builds
  choices automatically.
- Add `flashOnly:true` to long passages (creeds, etc.) so they only appear as flashcards, not quizzes.

- Add `core:true` to a card to put it in **Board Core** (the default Mock Board / cram / Learn set).

Bump `meta.version` at the top of `data/content.js` when you publish (it shows on the launch screen and under
More). After editing, raise the number in the `CACHE` name in **`sw.js`** (e.g. `ncoboard-v7` → `ncoboard-v8`)
so phones pick up the new content next time they're online, then re-upload the folder.

## A note on accuracy

Cards were written from the 18 source publications and checked page by page against the PDFs; most
cards cite the page they came from. Army publications change, so a few things to know:

- The fitness-test cards follow the **Army Fitness Test (AFT)**, the test of record since 1 June 2025
  (AR 350-1, June 2025; TC 7-21.13, September 2025).
- Two source PDFs are old editions: **ADP 3-0 (2011)** and **STP 21-24 (2008)**. Cards that taught
  replaced doctrine from them were rewritten from current publications or removed.
- Grooming cards include the 2025 Army Directives that changed AR 670-1: **AD 2025-13** (facial hair) and
  **AD 2025-18** (hair, cosmetics, nails, earrings, AGSU wear), plus the 2026 directives: **AD 2026-13** (body
  composition by waist-to-height ratio), **AD 2026-09** (sideburns, religious accommodation), and **AD 2026-07**
  (Combat Field Test). Each card cites its directive.
- Always follow your board's appointment memo and local SOP. Spot an error? Use the flag on any card.
